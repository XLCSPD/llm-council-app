## Why LLM Council?

Ever asked an AI a complex question and wondered if the answer was *really* the best one? We did too. So we built a council.

**Here's how it works:** You ask a question, and a team of AI models independently reason through it, critique each other's thinking, and synthesize a final answer with confidence levels. Think of it as a boardroom of AI experts debating on your behalf.

**The result?** Better decisions through structured disagreement – not just a single model's best guess.

## Getting Started (2 minutes)

### Step 1: Create Your Account
1. Go to **https://frontend-phi-black-hybffec8kj.vercel.app/**
2. Click the **"Create Account"** tab
3. Enter your email address and choose a password
4. Click **"Create Account"** – you're in!

*Prefer passwordless? Click **"Use magic link instead"** and we'll email you a sign-in link.*

### Step 2: Sign In (Next Time)
1. Go to the app URL above
2. On the **"Sign In"** tab, enter your email and password
3. Click **"Sign In"**

*Or use **"Use magic link instead"** to receive a sign-in link via email.*

### Step 3: Create Your First Session
1. Click **"New Session"** in the sidebar
2. Type your question or problem in the prompt box
3. (Optional) Add context, objectives, or constraints to guide the council

### Step 4: Assemble Your Council
1. Choose your AI models from the model selector
2. Assign roles: **Thinker**, **Critic**, **Devil's Advocate**, or **Chair**
3. Hit **"Start Deliberation"** and watch the magic happen

## The Four Phases

Your deliberation flows through:
1. **Setup** – Configure your prompt and council
2. **Reasoning** – Each model thinks independently
3. **Review** – Models critique and score each other
4. **Synthesis** – Chairman delivers the final verdict with confidence level
